# Design Approaches

Three distinct design directions were built for client review. Each lives on its own route and shares the same content — only the visual treatment differs.

## 1. FORGED — Dark Industrial Editorial (`/forged`)

**Concept:** Dark, textured editorial evoking raw mining materiality with corporate authority.

- **Palette:** Charcoal `#1A1A1E` / Card `#2A2A2F` / Copper `#C17F4E` / Warm white `#F0ECE4` / Muted `#8A8680`
- **Typography:** Instrument Serif (display) + DM Sans (body) + DM Mono (data)
- **Key Elements:** Film grain texture overlay, copper line-draw animations on scroll, word-by-word staggered hero headline, copper-bordered service cards, pulsing market dots
- **Hero:** Full viewport, headline words stagger in, animated copper line at 60% height, two CTAs (filled + ghost)
- **Mood:** Industrial authority — "We are established & serious"
- **Inspiration:** Trafigura dark corporate, BHP bold simplicity, architectural magazine editorials

## 2. TERRA — Warm Light Editorial (`/terra`)

**Concept:** Warm, paper-textured editorial with the feel of a premium consulting document.

- **Palette:** Cream `#F5F0E8` / Paper `#FEFCF8` / Copper `#B07A3E` / Espresso `#2C2419` / Slate `#6B6259`
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond (display, italic for quotes) + Libre Franklin (body)
- **Key Elements:** Topographic contour line backgrounds, copper offset photo frames (12px shift), italic pull-quotes with copper left border, alternating cream/paper sections
- **Hero:** Two-column (text + offset-framed image), pill-shaped CTA "Begin a Conversation"
- **Mood:** Refined trust — "We are trustworthy partners"
- **Inspiration:** McKinsey/BCG editorial feel, geological survey documents, premium print design

## 3. VEIN — Kinetic Data-Driven Brutalist (`/vein`)

**Concept:** Dark, data-forward interface inspired by commodity trading terminals and mining control rooms.

- **Palette:** Deep black `#0D0D0F` / Card `#161618` / Copper `#E8913A` / Teal `#2BBCB3` / Cool white `#E8E8EC`
- **Typography:** Bebas Neue (display) + Syne (body) + JetBrains Mono (data)
- **Key Elements:** Scrolling data ticker ribbons, animated counting numbers, pulsing status indicator dots, clip-path headline reveal, glow hover effects, grid overlay background
- **Hero:** Full viewport centered, "FROM MINE TO MARKET" clip-reveal, data ticker at bottom
- **Dual accent system:** Copper for mining operations, Teal for advisory/markets
- **Mood:** Technical precision — "We are technically capable"
- **Inspiration:** Bloomberg terminal aesthetics, commodity dashboards, brutalist web design
